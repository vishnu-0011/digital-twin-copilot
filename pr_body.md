### 🎯 Key Enhancements & Milestone Deliverables

#### 1. Facility Scale: 8 Distinct Aerospace Workcells across 4 Zoned Bays
* **Bay 1 (Machining Bay)**:
  * `CNC-01`: 5-Axis Heavy Roughing Mill (DMG MORI style enclosure, sliding glass doors, 6-tool carousel, chip auger chute & bin).
  * `CNC-02`: 5-Axis High-Speed Airfoil Finishing Center (granite portal bridge, tilting trunnion table, mist extraction filtration unit, Siemens 840D console).
* **Bay 2 (Forming & Thermal Bay)**:
  * `PRESS-01`: 1000-Ton Hydraulic Bulkhead Forging Press (tie-rod columns, nitrogen accumulators, fluid reservoir, ruby safety light curtains, hot titanium billet).
  * `PRESS-02`: 500-Ton Hydraulic Extrusion Press (horizontal tie-bars, ram, billet chamber, and runoff roller table carrying titanium spar extrusions).
  * `FURN-01`: Vacuum Carburizing Heat Treatment Furnace (heavy clamping vacuum door, glowing radiant orange quartz heat port, turbo-molecular pumping skid, 980°C controller).
* **Bay 3 (Robotics & Metrology Bay)**:
  * `ROBOT-01`: 6-DOF Industrial Articulated Robotic Arm inside yellow safety fencing with clear polycarbonate panels performing root-radius deburring on turbine blades.
  * `LASER-01`: Dual Laser Triangulation QC Arch on a black granite surface plate with sweeping cyan planar laser sheet and micrometer readout tower.
* **Bay 4 (Assembly Line Bay)**:
  * `CONV-01`: Modular Dual-Rail Avionics Assembly Line with emergency E-stop cords, enclosed laser QC inspection tunnel, and moving aerospace pallet fixtures.
* **Logistics Fleet**:
  * `AGV-01` & `AGV-02`: Coordinated Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) with rotating 3D LiDAR sensors, dynamic floor headlights, safety beacons, and cargo payloads.

#### 2. Executive Cleanroom Light Theme (HOD & Plant Executive Ready)
* **Default Light Cleanroom Aesthetic**: Crisp architectural daylight, polished white epoxy slab (`#f3f4f6`), high-contrast dark slate typography (`#0f172a`), translucent frosted HUD, and vibrant status badges.
* **Instant Dark Mode Switcher**: `☀️ Light / 🌙 Dark` button allows seamless toggling between the daytime cleanroom environment and the cyberpunk night strategy grid.
* **Theme-Adaptive Oscilloscope**: Live 60 FPS dual-channel oscilloscope automatically adjusts background, grid lines, and trace contrast for both light and dark themes.

#### 3. 10-Stage Process Pipeline (`GET /pipeline/flow`)
* Full end-to-end manufacturing flow covering: Raw Ingot Ingestion ➔ Roughing Milling ➔ Airfoil Finishing ➔ 1000T Forging ➔ 500T Extrusion ➔ Vacuum Carburizing ➔ Robotic Deburring ➔ Laser QC Metrology ➔ Avionics Assembly ➔ Cleanroom AMR Logistics.
* Interactive click-to-focus: clicking any manufacturing stage immediately directs the camera to that workcell and opens diagnostic telemetry.

#### 4. 100% Pure ML & Self-Contained Offline Architecture
* **2021 PHM Challenge Winner TCN**: 1D Dilated Causal Temporal Convolutional Network ($d = 1, 2, 4, 8$) trained locally without external cloud APIs.
* **Zero Feature Leakage**: Strictly observable sensors (`vibration_rms`, `temperature_c`, $\Delta\text{vib}$, $\Delta\text{temp}$).
* **15% Asymmetric Safety Buffer**: $\text{RUL}_{\text{safe}} = 0.85 \times \text{RUL}_{\text{pred}}$ preventing catastrophic spindle tool crashes.
* **Zero Node/NPM build overhead & zero API keys required**.

#### 5. Executive Cleanroom Entrance Portal & Unobstructed Perimeter
* **"TITAN AEROSPACE" Branding**: Dual-sided high-resolution illuminated signage rendered via dynamic HTML5 canvas texture with glowing cyan borders, aerospace chevron emblem, and ISO Class 6 operational tags.
* **Architectural Airlock**: Frameless sliding glass doors with brushed stainless steel handles, emerald green positive-pressure status indicator strip, and RFID security card pedestals.
* **Perimeter Declutter**: Removed all 12 perimeter structural columns and overhead gantry crane clutter, delivering an unencumbered, crystal-clear 3D RTS camera view.
* **Dedicated Director Preset**: Added a `🚪 Entrance` camera preset button in the Director bar to glide the viewport directly to the front airlock portal.

#### 6. Teammate Master Guide & Architectural Deep-Dive (`About.md`)
