# SOP-FRN-003: Vacuum Furnace Heating Element and Thermocouple Drift

**Applies to:** FURNACE class machines (FURN-01)
**Planned service:** about 20 minutes, between heat-treat batches only
**Signals used:** chamber temperature, fan and pump vibration, predicted remaining life (RUL)

## Symptom: Temperature creeping above setpoint, rising vibration
Ageing heating elements and drifting thermocouples make the furnace overshoot
its setpoint; worn circulation fan or vacuum pump bearings add vibration.
Out-of-tolerance heat treatment can make a whole batch of parts unusable.

**Recommended action:**
1. Service soon (safety-adjusted RUL under 60 cycles): schedule the service
   for the next gap between batches. Never interrupt a batch in progress.
2. Service now (safety-adjusted RUL under 30 cycles): do not load a new batch
   until the furnace has been serviced.

## Procedure
1. Let the batch finish; cool the chamber under vacuum; vent and lock out.
2. Inspect elements for sagging or hot spots; measure element resistance.
3. Check thermocouples against a reference probe; replace any reading more
   than 2 degrees C off.
4. Inspect fan and pump bearings; run a survey cycle and confirm the
   temperature uniformity before releasing the furnace.
