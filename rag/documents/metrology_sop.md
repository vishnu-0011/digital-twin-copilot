# SOP-QC-002: Laser Metrology Scan Head and Stage

**Applies to:** METROLOGY class machines (LASER-01)
**Planned service:** about 4 minutes (optics clean, stage check, recalibration)
**Signals used:** stage vibration, head temperature, predicted remaining life (RUL)

## Symptom: Rising stage vibration or head temperature
Contaminated optics and wear in the linear stage bearings make measurements
noisy. The danger is not a breakdown but wrong measurements: good parts
rejected, or bad parts passed.

**Recommended action:**
1. Service soon (safety-adjusted RUL under 60 cycles): clean and recalibrate
   at the next shift change.
2. Service now (safety-adjusted RUL under 30 cycles): hold the inspection
   queue; re-measure the last parts after recalibration.

## Procedure
1. Park the stage; cap the laser; lock out.
2. Clean the scan head window and mirrors with the approved lens tissue.
3. Check stage bearings for play; re-lubricate the guides.
4. Measure the reference artefact; recalibrate if outside tolerance and
   re-measure the parts inspected since the last good check.
