Deep Dive · Pillar 1

The "Why" Page Explained: The Problem We Solve

Located at /why.html, this page addresses the fundamental business and human factors failure modes in industrial manufacturing.

Key Concepts on the Why Page

  • The P-F Warning Interval: Point P is when micro-vibrations become detectable; Point F is functional failure. Plants without copilots only react after Point F.
  • The $400/Minute Downtime Trap: A seized 5-axis mill stops the deburring robot, the vacuum furnace, and avionics assembly behind it.
  • The Human Factors Alarm Fatigue: Standard SCADA systems fire 200+ raw thresholds per shift. Operators experience alarm numbness and hit mute.
  • Why Aerospace: Inconel and titanium alloys wear tools unpredictably based on lot hardness, rendering fixed hour-based calendars useless.
🎓 Evaluator / Viva Question for the Why Page:

"Why can't plants simply install traditional SCADA thresholds (e.g. if vibration > 5.0 mm/s then alarm)?"

Student Defense: Single-point scalar thresholds fire too late (when bearing pitting has already propagated) and cannot estimate Remaining Useful Life (RUL). Furthermore, raw alarms lack actionable root-cause procedures, creating cognitive overload.

Deep Dive · Pillar 2

The "How It Works" Page Explained: Architecture & Math

Located at /method.html, this page documents the four-tier predictive pipeline, strict temporal causality, and mathematical formulations.

4-Tier Engineering Architecture

  • Tier 1 · SimPy Physics Engine: Simulates non-linear tool wear, bearing raceway degradation, and Poisson workcell delays with strict zero-feature-leakage.
  • Tier 2 · Dilated TCN Prognostics: 1D causal convolutions with exponentially growing dilation sequence d ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8} yielding large receptive fields without RNN bottlenecks.
  • Tier 3 · Strict Causality via Chomp-1D: Custom PyTorch layer removes right-side padding so the model never sees future states.
  • Tier 4 · ISO 10816-3 & Deterministic SOPs: Classifies vibration velocity into Zones A (Good) to D (Unacceptable) and retrieves verified shop SOP checklists via TF-IDF cosine similarity.
🎓 Evaluator / Viva Question for the Method Page:

"Why choose a Temporal Convolutional Network over an LSTM or Transformer?"

Student Defense: TCN trains in parallel O(1) across time steps (unlike sequential O(N) LSTMs), avoids vanishing gradients via residual connections, and has mathematically bounded receptive fields. It runs 100% offline in milliseconds on standard edge CPUs.

Deep Dive · Pillar 3

The "Results" Page Explained: Empirical Validation

Located at /results.html, this page presents benchmark proof scored via python -m evaluation.run_all on both simulated cleanrooms and NASA C-MAPSS data.

Measured Benchmark Highlights

  • NASA C-MAPSS FD001 Comparison: TCN achieved 7.0 cycle RMSE and 5.3 cycle MAE, outperforming standard Bi-LSTM (14.2 RMSE) and Random Forest (18.6 RMSE).
  • 68% Reduction in Unplanned Stoppages: Policy simulation over 1,000 production hours demonstrated cutting catastrophic seizures from 12 down to 4.
  • $140,000+ Downtime Cost Reduction: Replacing emergency repairs with pre-scheduled off-shift servicing drastically cut overtime labor and scrapped titanium billets.
  • Reproducible Science: Every metric and chart is generated deterministically from evaluation/results.json with zero hardcoded claims.
🎓 Evaluator / Viva Question for the Results Page:

"How was the policy simulation evaluated without bias?"

Student Defense: We subjected the exact same randomized machine wear seeds to three distinct controllers: Run-to-Failure (replace after seizure), Fixed Calendar (service every 150 cycles), and Copilot Predictive (service when RUL < 30 with 0.85 safety margin).
